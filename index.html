Pricing Policy
⚡
EV — Lowest Rate
Electric vehicles receive the maximum discount to promote clean and eco-friendly transportation on our toll network.
🔋
Hybrid — Concession Rate
Hybrid vehicles receive a mid-range concession — lower than standard but above EV rates, reflecting partial electric operation.
⛽
Fuel / ICE — Standard Rate
Standard rate applies to all petrol and diesel vehicles. Rates are updated by the toll authority periodically.
